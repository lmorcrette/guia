History

Paris Elysées was created in France in 1990 to bring French perfumery within everyone's reach. Its first major international success was Dollar (1991), an aromatic fougère for men. The same idea runs through the Billion Dollar line (2006) and up to Billion Dollar Elixir (2026): more than thirty years of one signature.

The brand's formulas are created in Grasse, the historic capital of French perfumery. Since 2005, the group has produced its perfumes in its own factory in India.

In Brazil, Paris Elysées has had an official presence since 2000. 2025 was the brand's best year in Brazil.

Original creations

Paris Elysées does not compare its perfumes with those of other brands. Each perfume is a creation of the brand, with its own name, formula and olfactory pyramid.

Frequently asked questions

Is Paris Elysées a French brand?

Yes. Paris Elysées is a French perfume house, created in France in 1990, with formulas developed in Grasse. The group has its own factory in India since 2005.

Where are Paris Elysées perfumes made?

The formulas are created in Grasse, France. The perfumes are produced in the group's own factory in India, in operation since 2005.

Are Paris Elysées perfumes copies of other brands?

No. Paris Elysées does not compare its perfumes with those of other brands: each perfume is its own creation, with its own formula and olfactory pyramid.

Where can I buy original Paris Elysées perfumes in Brazil?
