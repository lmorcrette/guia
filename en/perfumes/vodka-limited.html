Vodka LimitedMen's woody / aromatic fougère fragrance · Eau de Toilette · 100 ml

Vodka Limited is a men's woody / aromatic fougère fragrance by Paris Elysées, the French perfume house founded in 1990, with its formula created in Grasse. It opens with bergamot and lavender, has a heart of coriander and geranium and a base of cedar and amber.
