Night CaviarMen's woody / amber (oriental) fragrance · Eau de Toilette · 100 ml

Night Caviar is a men's woody / amber (oriental) fragrance by Paris Elysées, the French perfume house founded in 1990, with its formula created in Grasse. It opens with absinthe, grapefruit and pineapple, has a heart of lavender, artemisia and anise and a base of black vanilla, musk, vetiver and patchouli.
