Billion Casino RoyalMen's woody / amber (oriental) fragrance · Eau de Toilette · 100 ml

Billion Casino Royal is a men's woody / amber (oriental) fragrance by Paris Elysées, the French perfume house founded in 1990, with its formula created in Grasse. It opens with orange blossom, bergamot and lemon, has a heart of lavender, cardamom and geranium and a base of tonka bean, lychee, teak wood and vetiver.
