Paris Elysées · Billion Collection

Billion Exclusive Men's woody / gourmand fragrance · Eau de Toilette · 100 ml

Billion Exclusive is a men's woody / gourmand fragrance by Paris Elysées, the French perfume house founded in 1990, with its formula created in Grasse. It opens with green apple and davana, has a heart of turkish rose, osmanthus and tonka bean and a base of vanilla, almond, cedar and patchouli.
